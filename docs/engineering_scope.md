# Engineering Scope

Demonstrates multistage administrative-event modeling, SQLite checkpoint audits, cross-table reconciliation, nested cohort accounting, process-clock validation, relational QA, deterministic reporting, automated tests, CI, and Docker. The private empirical analysis is not reproduced.

The upgrade adds offline replay of a declared single ordered delivery stream, event/arrival-time separation, revision and retraction handling, as-of projections, late-update retention, completed/open clock separation, frozen-reference duration anomaly candidates, and a review queue with timestamp/revision evidence.

It is a synthetic toy single-pass workflow, not the scientific project's accepted process specification. Business calendars, legal deadlines, organizational responsibility, broker integration, distributed checkpoint recovery, deployment-scale performance and external detector calibration are outside the demonstrated scope.
