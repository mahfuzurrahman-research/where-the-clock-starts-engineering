# Scientific Boundary

All records are synthetic. Passing software checks demonstrates software behavior only. The repository does not establish negligence, wrongdoing, legal breach, causal effects, or private-study findings.

The stream's five-stage single-pass path is a declared engineering convention. A repeated stage may be a legitimate process in a real program; here it prevents an unambiguous toy clock. Source absence does not establish actual nonperformance. Calendar elapsed hours and open ages do not establish business-day lateness or a legal obligation.

The historical median/MAD detector and all thresholds are synthetic engineering choices. Scenario labels test intended software behavior; they do not estimate sensitivity, specificity, fairness, calibration, or predictive performance on an external population. Every queue entry remains `review_required`, with legal-breach and wrongdoing claims set to false.

Source and artifact hashes are unsigned consistency checks. They do not authenticate provenance or certify that arbitrary externally supplied records are synthetic. The shipped generator fabricates every value without accessing private assets.
